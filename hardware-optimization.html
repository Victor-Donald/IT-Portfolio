PROJECT OVERVIEW

Endpoint Hardware & Storage Maintenance

This project focused on improving endpoint reliability and responsiveness through targeted hardware and storage maintenance.

Work included internal SSD replacement, disk partitioning, storage troubleshooting, and hardware diagnostics across multiple computer systems.

The project provided hands-on experience identifying performance bottlenecks and determining whether a hardware upgrade, storage adjustment, or other remediation was appropriate.
